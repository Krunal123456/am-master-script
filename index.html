How it works

Open Pipeline is the master dataset. The application does not modify the original Open Pipeline data.

Each opportunity is checked against the AM Stale Opps report. If the opportunity exists in the stale report, Stale = YES is added and highlighted in red.

After processing, the system creates:

  • Consolidated Master Script Excel
  • Individual AM Excel reports
  • Google Drive folder structure
  • Individual email reports
